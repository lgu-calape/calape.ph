Nurturing Young Minds Since 1917

A Century of Excellence in Education

Welcome to Calape Central Elementary School!

Located in the heart of Sta. Cruz, Calape, Bohol, Calape Central Elementary School has been a cornerstone of quality education in our community for over a century. Established in 1917, we are proud of our rich history of fostering academic excellence, character development, and a love for learning in generations of students.

Our Legacy of Learning

At Calape Central Elementary School, we believe in providing a nurturing and stimulating environment where every child can thrive. Our dedicated educators are committed to:

  • Academic Excellence: Delivering a comprehensive curriculum that challenges and inspires students to reach their full potential.
  • Holistic Development: Focusing on the intellectual, social, emotional, and physical growth of each child.
  • Community Spirit: Instilling values of respect, cooperation, and civic responsibility.
  • Foundational Knowledge: Equipping students with essential skills for future success.

Why Choose Calape Central Elementary School?

  • A Century of Excellence: With a legacy dating back to 1917, we have a proven track record of educational success.
  • Dedicated Educators: Our passionate and experienced teachers are committed to guiding and inspiring young learners.
  • Supportive Environment: We provide a safe, inclusive, and encouraging atmosphere for all students.
  • Community Focused: We are deeply rooted in the Calape community, fostering strong partnerships between home and school.
