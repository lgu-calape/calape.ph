About Calape National High School

Calape National High School is dedicated to providing quality education that fosters academic excellence, character development, and civic responsibility. We strive to create a nurturing and challenging environment where every student can achieve their full potential and become a productive member of society. Our committed faculty and staff work tirelessly to inspire a love for learning and prepare students for success in higher education and beyond.

History of Calape National High School

Calape National High School's history is rooted in its establishment as an annex of Pangangan High School. The school's transition to an independent national high school was formalized through Republic Act No. 9810. This act, passed in 2009, officially separated the Calape Annex from Pangangan High School, recognizing the growing needs of the Calape community. The separation ensured dedicated resources and focused attention on the students of Calape. The act mandated the transfer of personnel, assets, and records to the newly independent Calape National High School.
